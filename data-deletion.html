Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how to request deletion or correction of information connected to an Olea Tax Co. inquiry. It is designed to remain useful for platform dashboards even while the website is a static draft.

Current Website Data Storage

  • The public website is static.
  • The site does not currently provide login, account creation, payments, public comments, or a website database.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics measures aggregate page views and website performance for visitors outside the European Union. Olea Tax Co. does not use it to identify visitors or connect browsing activity with an inquiry.
  • Inquiry information is sent only when you choose to contact Olea Tax Co. through email or another linked third-party service.
  • Olea Tax Co. uses information only for legitimate, disclosed purposes and does not sell it or use it for unrelated advertising.

How to Request Deletion

Email hello@oleataxco.com with the subject line "Data deletion request." Include the email address, name, and platform or contact method you used so the request can be matched to the correct records.

Olea Tax Co. will review the request and delete or correct eligible inquiry information when possible. Some records may need to be retained when required for legal, tax, professional, or business-record obligations.

Platform-Controlled Data

If you contacted Olea Tax Co. through a social platform, email provider, scheduling tool, or other third-party service, you may also need to use that platform's privacy or account tools to manage data controlled by that provider.

Future Intake Tools

If the site later adds direct-submission forms, materially different analytics, bookings, payments, accounts, or platform app integrations, these instructions will be replaced with a more specific deletion workflow.
