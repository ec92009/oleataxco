Privacy Policy

Olea Tax Co. uses this static website to explain services and invite initial inquiries. The site itself does not provide account registration, payments, public comments, or a website database. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics as described below.

Website Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregate page-view and website-performance measurements. Olea Tax Co. has configured Cloudflare not to collect these browser measurements from visitors in the European Union. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not log URL query strings, and Olea Tax Co. does not use these measurements to identify visitors or connect browsing activity with an inquiry.

Information You Choose to Send

If you contact Olea Tax Co. by email or another linked service, you may choose to provide your name, contact details, business context, and a short description of the tax or advisory support you are seeking.

Please do not send tax documents, Social Security numbers, bank details, or other sensitive financial information through social DMs or ordinary first-contact messages. If an engagement moves forward, Kelly will direct you to the appropriate secure document-sharing process.

How Inquiry Information Is Used

Olea Tax Co. collects and uses information only for legitimate, disclosed purposes connected to website security and measurement, responding to an inquiry, providing requested services, or meeting legal and professional record obligations. Inquiry information is not sold or used for unrelated advertising.

  • To review whether Olea Tax Co. may be a fit for your needs.
  • To reply to your inquiry and recommend a next step.
  • To maintain ordinary business records if you become a client.

Third-Party Services

Cloudflare provides website hosting, security, and analytics. Email providers, social platforms, secure document tools, and any future scheduling or intake tools have their own privacy practices. Information you send through those services is handled by those providers as well as by Olea Tax Co.

Deletion or Correction

To request deletion or correction of inquiry information you previously sent, email hello@oleataxco.com. Some records may need to be retained when required for legal, tax, professional, or business-record obligations.

Future Changes

This policy will be updated if the site adds direct-submission forms, payments, accounts, advertising pixels, a database, or materially different analytics.
