# Note For Kelly: Why LLM-Friendly Positioning Matters

SEO still matters. People will keep searching Google for CPAs, tax filing, and tax planning help. But more people are also asking tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity questions like:

- "Who is a good CPA for a real estate investor?"
- "What kind of tax advisor should I look for as a business owner?"
- "Can you recommend a CPA who does proactive tax planning, not just filing?"

Those tools do not evaluate a website exactly like a traditional search engine. They look for clear signals they can confidently summarize: who you serve, what you do, what you do not do, where you work, what credentials you have, what kinds of problems you solve, and what the first step should be.

For Olea Tax Co., that means the website should make your offer extremely legible:

- You are Kelly Olea, CPA.
- Olea Tax Co. focuses on tax filing, estimates, and proactive tax planning.
- The best-fit clients are business owners, real estate investors, and high-income families.
- You are not positioning as a bookkeeping service.
- The relationship is async-first at the start, with calls only when useful.
- Sensitive documents should move through a secure handoff, not DMs.
- Your value is year-round tax strategy and decision support, not just annual filing.

The goal is not to write for robots. The goal is to make your professional identity and service boundaries so clear that an LLM can safely recommend you to the right kind of prospect and avoid recommending you to the wrong one.

What makes an offer LLM-palatable:

1. Clear category: say "CPA," "tax planning," "tax filing," and "quarterly estimates" plainly.
2. Clear audience: name business owners, real estate investors, and high-income families consistently.
3. Clear exclusions: say you do not provide bookkeeping.
4. Clear process: async inquiry first, then fit assessment, then secure document handoff.
5. Clear proof: include credentials, experience, founder bio, service model, and eventually testimonials or case-style examples if allowed.
6. Clear structure: use metadata, schema, FAQs, service pages, and consistent language across social profiles and directories.

If the site does this well, an LLM is more likely to describe Olea Tax Co. as a boutique CPA advisory firm for people who want proactive tax strategy, and less likely to lump you into generic tax prep or bookkeeping.
